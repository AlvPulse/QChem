# Deductive Action Plan: A Comprehensive Mechanistic Analysis of Quantum Level 8

This action plan focuses exclusively on rigorously dissecting our positive result: the **Level 8 (Level G)** architecture. Our goal is to prove *why* the structural inductive bias generated by the topological measurement readout ($\sum A_{ij} \langle Z_i Z_j \rangle$) works, isolating the exact source of its advantage over classical parameter-matched baselines.

We achieve this through two main pillars:
1. **Core Ablation Studies** (Isolating the mechanism)
2. **The Six Dimensions of Representation Quality** (Benchmarking the resulting bias)

---

## Pillar 1: Core Ablation Studies (The "Why")

To prove that Level 8's success is a fundamentally *quantum* advantage driven by physical entanglement, we must systematically destroy the architecture to see when the performance collapses.

### Phase A: The Entanglement Ablation (Quantum Utilization)
**Hypothesis:** The structural readout bias only works if the underlying Hilbert space state is physically correlated. If we remove the entangling operations, the readout is just polling independent qubits, and performance will collapse to (or below) classical baselines.
**The Probe (`probe_entanglement_harvesting.py`):**
Compare the Inductive Bias Gap (Structured - Scrambled AUC) across three configurations:
*   `Level 8 (Full)`: Graph-gated $IsingXX$ placement + bond-pooled readout.
*   `No Entanglement (Separable)`: Remove all $IsingXX$/$CRZ$ gates, but keep the bond-pooled readout. *If this performs well, the "quantum" part is useless.*
*   `Gate Only (Standard Readout)`: Keep the graph-gated entanglement but use standard single-qubit $\langle Z_i \rangle$ readouts.

### Phase B: Information Preservation vs. Over-smoothing
**Hypothesis:** Classical models (even parameter-matched) suffer from "over-smoothing" as they aggregate this structure via message passing. The quantum model's unitary evolution preserves distinct, topology-aligned variances.
**The Probe (`probe_oversmoothing.py`):** Extract the latent representations of the clusters *immediately after* the quantum circuit (Level 8) and after the classical message-passing block (`ClassicalGNN_pm`). Compute pairwise Cosine Similarity to prove classical homogenization.

## Pillar 2: The Six Dimensions of Representation Quality (The "How Well")

Beyond the core mechanism, we will measure the *quality* of the Level 8 representation across six distinct dimensions to prove its superiority.

### Dimension 1: Sample Efficiency (کارایی نمونه)
*   **The Check:** Measure how fast the representation improves as data scales.
*   **The Probe:** Plot the Learning Curve ($\Delta$AUC vs. Training Fraction $0.1 \rightarrow 1.0$) for Level 8 vs `ClassicalGNN_pm`.

### Dimension 2: Trainability (آموزش‌پذیری)
*   **The Check:** Measure the smoothness of the optimization landscape (avoiding Barren Plateaus and Kernel Concentration).
*   **The Probe (`probe_kernel_concentration.py` & `probe_gradient_variance.py`):** Track the gradient variance $\text{Var}(\nabla \theta)$ and Gram matrix off-diagonal variance as $K$ scales.

### Dimension 3: Robustness (استواری)
*   **The Check:** Measure stability against hardware noise and input perturbations.
*   **The Probe (`probe_noise_resilience.py`):** Inject 0% - 20% depolarizing noise (Figure 16 confirmation) to prove the fixed $A_{ij}$ structural readout creates a noise-resilient prior.

### Dimension 4: OOD Generalization (تعمیم خارج از توزیع)
*   **The Check:** Measure the ability to transfer to unseen chemical scaffolds.
*   **The Probe:** Compute the Generalization Gap (Train AUC - Test AUC) comparing Random Splits vs. strict Scaffold Splits.

### Dimension 5: Parameter Efficiency (کارایی پارامتر)
*   **The Check:** Measure the representational power extracted per parameter.
*   **The Probe:** Handled implicitly by the strict dynamic parameter matching in the classical baseline (`ClassicalGNN_pm`), ensuring any Level 8 win is a victory of efficiency, not capacity.

### Dimension 6: Interpretability (تفسیرپذیری)
*   **The Check:** Measure the alignment of the representation with grounded chemical concepts.
*   **The Probe (`probe_attention.py`):** Extract the raw $\langle Z_i Z_j \rangle$ correlators scaled by $A_{ij}$ to perform an edge-level attribution (SHAP equivalent), visualizing exactly which functional groups the circuit relied upon for prediction.

## Conclusion
By executing this plan, we do not just claim "Level 8 is better." We explicitly prove *that entanglement is required* (Pillar 1), and we benchmark *exactly how the resulting representation is superior* across six comprehensive dimensions of ML theory (Pillar 2).
