# Deductive Action Plan: The Six Dimensions of Representation Quality in Quantum Level 8

This action plan focuses *exclusively* on evaluating and improving our **Level 8 (Level G)** architecture. Instead of broad benchmarking against other levels, we will rigorously dissect the structural inductive bias generated by our topological measurement readout ($\sum A_{ij} \langle Z_i Z_j \rangle$).

To prove that Level 8 provides a Nature-level contribution, we will evaluate its representation quality across the **Six Dimensions of Representation Quality**.

---

## Dimension 1: Sample Efficiency (کارایی نمونه)
**Definition:** The speed at which the representation improves as data increases.
**The Hypothesis:** Because Level 8 embeds chemical topology directly into the physical readout (rather than requiring parameters to learn it), it drastically reduces the hypothesis space. It will achieve comparable performance with 10% of the training data relative to a classical model that must learn structural aggregation from scratch.
**The Probe:** Plot the Learning Curve ($\Delta$AUC vs. Training Fraction $0.1 \rightarrow 1.0$).

## Dimension 2: Trainability (آموزش‌پذیری)
**Definition:** The smoothness of the optimization landscape, specifically avoiding Barren Plateaus.
**The Hypothesis:** Standard QML models suffer from vanishing gradients as $K$ (qubits) increases. Level 8's bond-pooled 2-local readouts preserve local graph information, shielding the circuit from global Barren Plateaus.
**The Probe (`probe_gradient_variance.py`):** Track the gradient variance $\text{Var}(\nabla \theta)$ of the graph-gated parameters across $K=4, 6, 8, 10$. If variance decays polynomially instead of exponentially, Trainability is proven.

## Dimension 3: Robustness (استواری)
**Definition:** The stability of the representation against input perturbations and hardware noise.
**The Hypothesis:**
1. *Hardware:* The structural readout is highly resilient to depolarizing noise (as seen in Figure 16) because the structural bias relies on the fixed $A_{ij}$ rather than delicate amplitudes.
2. *Chemical:* Small chemical perturbations (e.g., swapping a halogen) will not drastically shift the latent representation because the macroscopic topology $A$ remains stable.
**The Probe (`probe_noise_resilience.py`):** Inject 0% - 20% depolarizing noise and measure the decay of the Structured vs. Scrambled performance gap. Evaluate the kernel concentration variance under noise.

## Dimension 4: OOD Generalization (تعمیم خارج از توزیع)
**Definition:** The ability of the representation to transfer to unseen chemical scaffolds.
**The Hypothesis:** Classical models easily memorize the training scaffolds. Level 8 acts as a structural regularizer, forcing the model to rely on fundamental bond interactions rather than memorized sub-structures, leading to a massive advantage on strict Murcko Scaffold splits.
**The Probe:** Compute the Generalization Gap (Train AUC - Test AUC) comparing Random Splits vs. strict Scaffold Splits. Level 8 should show a vastly smaller drop on the Scaffold split.

## Dimension 5: Parameter Efficiency (کارایی پارامتر)
**Definition:** The amount of representational power extracted per parameter.
**The Hypothesis:** While a classical model requires large matrices to compute attention or message passing, Level 8 utilizes the Hilbert space geometry (unitaries) to process the graph. Even with tightly constrained parameter counts (e.g., $K=8$), Level 8 generates a significantly higher effective dimension than a parameter-matched classical model (`ClassicalGNN_pm`).
**The Probe (`probe_expressivity.py`):** Compute the Effective Dimension or Spectral Gap of the Level 8 Quantum Kernel vs. the Classical PM baseline. Show that Level 8 occupies a more complex hypothesis space using the exact same parameter budget.

## Dimension 6: Interpretability (تفسیرپذیری)
**Definition:** The alignment of the representation with grounded chemical concepts.
**The Hypothesis:** The Level 8 representation is not a black box. Because the final logits are built from $\sum A_{ij} \langle Z_i Z_j \rangle$, we can perfectly trace back exactly which chemical bonds (edges in $A$) drove the toxicity prediction. Furthermore, the circuit attends distinctly to 3D properties (like electronegativity) if we encode them in the node features.
**The Probe (`probe_attention.py`):** Implement edge-level SHAP/Attribution by extracting the raw $\langle Z_i Z_j \rangle$ correlators scaled by $A_{ij}$. Visualize which functional groups the quantum circuit relied upon for classification.

## Action Items for Execution
All future coding efforts will strictly target one of these 6 dimensions for **Level 8 only**. We will ignore improvements to Levels 1-7 and focus entirely on solidifying this six-dimensional proof of Level 8's superiority.
